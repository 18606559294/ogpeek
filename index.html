Why this exists

01

Broken cards cost clicks

A missing og:image or a relative image path makes your link render as a sad text-only block. Studies put the click-through lift from a good preview at 2–5×.

02

Every platform is different

X trims at 1.4× ratio. Facebook wants 1.91:1. Discord renders in a dark embed. Slack shows a 75px thumb. ogpeek shows all of them, side by side, in one pass.

03

No accounts, no uploads

Your HTML never leaves your browser. There's no backend to log into and nothing to leak. Paste, read, fix, ship.
